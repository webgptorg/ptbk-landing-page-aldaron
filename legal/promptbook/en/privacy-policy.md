This policy describes which personal data we process when you visit ptbk.io or pavolhejny.cz, pavolhejny.com and ai-ta-krajta.cz or use the Promptbook services, why we do it, and which rights you have.

## 1. Accounts, licenses, referrals and article proposals

We record verified email, account identifier, trials, license parameters and assigned community seats to handle the request and perform the contract. The member area can securely link verified identity to the control app. The app receives necessary identifiers and capacities, not community-seat lists or engine working content.

We retain referral attribution and its history to establish deal provenance and settle the partner relationship. Discount codes are recorded separately. After opening a referral link, the browser remembers only its code and expiry for at most 90 days. The code can be corrected or cleared before purchase. This does not create marketing consent.

Article proposals contain name, email, topic, description and an optional draft link. We use these to handle the proposal and communicate with the author; submission does not publish an article or subscribe anyone to a newsletter. Requests use the same contact records and Twenty CRM with their purpose and provenance. The purpose-specific retention periods below apply.

manGoweb supplies software; that role does not itself grant routine access to contacts or engine content. Any service access is limited in advance to its necessary purpose and scope, with appropriate permissions and contractual arrangements.

## 4. Why we process the data

Every processing has its purpose and its legal basis.

- To answer you and agree on working together - based on pre-contractual negotiations and our legitimate interest in replying to an enquiry.
- To register you for a workshop or another event, issue an invoice, and send you the materials - based on the performance of the contract and our legal obligations.
- To verify subscription details, fulfil your order, keep track of payments and provide support, based on steps before entering into the contract, its performance and our legal obligations. For business contacts of company representatives, we also rely on our legitimate interest in administering and documenting the relationship.
- To deliver sign-in codes and verify access to your subscription – based on contract performance and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.
- To send you news - based on your consent, which you may withdraw at any time.
- To understand how the website works and what visitors care about - based on your consent to analytics cookies.
- To measure the effectiveness of our advertising - based on your consent to marketing cookies.
- To meet our legal obligations, mainly accounting and tax ones.

## 5. How long we keep the data

- Data from enquiry and registration forms for the duration of the negotiations and then three years from the last contact.
- Subscription information for the duration of the agreement and then to the extent and for as long as necessary to comply with legal obligations or defend legal claims.
- Accounting documents for ten years, as the law requires.
- The contact for sending news until you withdraw your consent.
- Data in analytics and marketing tools according to the settings of the given tool, at most 26 months.

## 8. AI models and working content

When you use an AI feature we provide, your input and necessary context may be sent to the model provider actually used by that feature, particularly OpenAI. Connections in an independent customer engine may also use Anthropic or another provider selected in its settings. Naming a provider does not mean that it receives every contact or is used in every deployment.

We distinguish an AI feature we operate, processing on a customer’s documented instructions, and direct model calls under the customer’s own account. The second route requires the appropriate processing agreement and subprocessor list identifying the actual service, contracting entity, purpose, data categories, location, retention and applicable mechanism for transfers outside the EEA. Customers ordinarily select and contract with providers for their direct accounts; this does not exclude our own separate processing where applicable.

Internal use of a model for development or advertising does not itself authorise sending CRM personal data or customer project content. Access and disclosure must follow the purpose and actual roles. Supplier and processing-route information must be available before the relevant transfer; a generic list of brands does not replace the necessary agreement and transparency.
