- **One coherent interactive area:** integrate a prominent visual story near the hero/use-case area, rather than attaching an unrelated demo below a finished marketing page. Let the visitor select website/application care, customer communication or accounting supporting documents using plainly labelled controls. Selection changes the visible work, result and benefit, not merely a selected border.
    - Reuse the homepage's localized scenario data and add only the presentation states needed here. Default to the software-maintenance example. An initial still state must already communicate the outcome; no introductory wait, login, form submission or puzzle is required to understand it.
    - Illustrate the same ongoing responsibility at more than one moment. A compact story progression can show an incoming need, work being handled, an outcome and a later related need. Offer simple forward/back or equivalent direct state controls and replay. Choose the clearest composition; do not build a general simulator or a dense dashboard.
    - Make the before/after difference visually legible: with one-off assistance the owner has to ask again; with an entrusted agenda relevant work continues within the agreed scope. A static side-by-side comparison or simple comparison control can carry this without forcing every scenario through a second complex interface.
    - Distinguish routine continuation from decisions still needed from the owner. Include at least one clear exception/approval state and an appropriate settled/waiting state. Do not show an infinite stream of invented tasks or imply that autonomy means working when nothing useful is needed.
- **Scenario stories and their limits:** show recognizable business artifacts and short outcome labels, not terminals or the framework's internal agent/task hierarchy. Use fictional demonstration content without real customer records, personal details or fabricated testimonials.
    - **Website/application:** a site/application preview has a reported issue; the story progresses to a checked proposed improvement, then shows continued care when another relevant need appears. Communicate less manual coordination and a maintained product. Distinguish any deployment stage from preparation/checking; do not visually claim an unapproved change is already live.
    - **Customer communication:** a recurring customer question leads to a prepared answer and an improved explanation for future enquiries; an unusual request goes to a person. Communicate less repeated handling and more consistent follow-through. If the illustration includes sending, show it as a separate explicitly authorized step rather than equating a draft with an external action.
    - **Accounting supporting documents:** a new document joins an organized set; a missing detail or mismatch becomes a concrete request for resolution; the result is better-prepared material for responsible review. Do not animate a real tax filing, payment or guaranteed professional accounting outcome.
    - Outcomes and any small approval/illustration labels must remain understandable in both languages. Do not rely solely on icon color to distinguish handled, pending and needs-attention states.
- **Interaction state:** use one deterministic state model for selected scenario, selected story step and any animation progress. The selected control, visible artifact, explanatory sentence and benefit must agree at all times.
    - Switching scenarios resets to a deliberate understandable state and cancels stale timers/transitions; rapid clicks must not mix the previous scenario's data with the new one. Going backward or replaying does not create duplicate cards/events.
    - Keep the scenario selector and progression usable by mouse, touch and keyboard with visible focus, meaningful accessible names and selected-state semantics. Changing a story does not hijack page focus, jump the viewport, submit an enquiry or navigate away.
    - Keep the primary business CTA easy to reach around the experience, using the already working homepage flow. Opening or closing it must not erase form inputs or unintentionally advance the story. Browsing a use case is never treated as permission to submit contact data.
    - These are client-side illustrative interactions only: no real agent execution, email sending, accounting operations, repo changes, external service connections, model calls or fake claim that the visitor has launched a live agenda. Identify the demonstration succinctly without covering it in disclaimers.
