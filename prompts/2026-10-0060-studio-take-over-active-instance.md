[✨🗝️] Take over the active Studio from another tab without finding or closing the original tab

- Preserve the existing single-active-instance principle. When Studio is active in tab A and blocked in tab B, the administrator must be able to deactivate A and take control in B entirely from B, even when A is hidden among many windows or on another desktop. Do not close, navigate or reload A's browser tab.
- Verified entry points: `lib/recording-studio/recordingStudioLock.ts`, `components/recording-studio/useRecordingStudio.ts`, `components/recording-studio/RecordingStudio.tsx`, `lib/recording-studio/RecordingStudioCapture.ts` and `lib/recording-studio/recordingStudioStorage.ts`. Today `useRecordingStudio` holds the exclusive browser lock for the page lifetime, and an unsuccessful acquisition tells the user to close the other tab and refresh.
- Add `Převzít studio v této kartě` beside that blocking message. Opening or focusing B never takes over automatically. Require confirmation in B, with explicit warning that the other instance may be recording and that takeover stops its recording and other active work; already committed material must be preserved, but an interrupted operation may lose its uncommitted tail. Show A's current state when obtainable, otherwise say the state is unknown.
- Implement an explicit, acknowledged ownership handover. Choose an appropriate same-origin coordination mechanism, for example BroadcastChannel alongside the browser lock; the exact mechanism is the implementer's decision. Scope it to the same origin, browser profile and storage partition, not other devices or unrelated applications.
    - Address the current owner using stable instance/request identities. Ignore stale, duplicate and unrelated messages. Two requesting tabs must not both become owners.
    - A responsive A stops accepting new work, settles an in-progress Start/pause/resume transition, stops all recorders, drains the bounded persistence queue and final data events, and preserves the completed or interrupted take with an accurate reason. It releases its camera, microphone and screen-capture tracks, including preview/cloned tracks.
    - Settle or cancel exports, derived-media jobs and uploads safely, preserving committed media and resumable upload metadata. Flush valid pending editor saves through the existing save queue. A failed flush must be reported to B; do not silently discard edits or claim a clean handover.
    - Only after cleanup and release of the actual exclusive lock may B become active and reload authoritative state. B must not recover, delete or overwrite A's take while A is still finalizing it. Do not substitute a local UI boolean for actual lock acquisition.
    - A stays open in a clearly deactivated state, with recording, editing and destructive controls disabled. It cannot silently reacquire ownership on focus, polling, a stale callback or refresh. It may offer the same explicit takeover action to regain control later.
    - B becomes usable without a manual page refresh. No media stream is magically transferred between tabs: reuse saved source configuration, but reacquire devices and show the browser's screen-selection dialog when required. Never automatically start a new recording after takeover.
- Show a bounded waiting state rather than an endless spinner. Handle A closing/crashing during handover, late acknowledgements, a cancelled requester and rejected cleanup. A background or hidden tab is not by itself a dead owner. Cancelling before handover leaves A unchanged; once stopping has begun, explain that cancellation cannot resume the old take automatically.
- Include recovery for an unresponsive owner. After a timeout, an explicitly confirmed forced takeover may revoke its authority only with a mechanism that prevents the old instance from writing after B takes control, including after A later resumes.
    - A storage-validated ownership generation/fencing token or equivalent must guard mutations at commit time, not merely before an asynchronous operation. Cover media writes, recovery/deletion, editor saves and any finalization/publication affected by ownership. Use the same authority model rather than an unrelated second lock.
    - Calling Web Locks with `steal: true` alone is insufficient: it does not stop the old callback. Do not treat a stale heartbeat as permission for two writers. If safe revocation cannot be established, keep B blocked with a concrete error/retry path rather than claiming success.
    - Distinguish logical ownership revocation from physically stopping a frozen tab's camera or microphone. A must stop its capture as soon as it can execute again; do not falsely report that the browser has killed an unresponsive tab or guaranteed an unflushed tail was saved.
- Keep one ownership boundary for recording, recovery and editing. When [the two-section Studio](2026-10-0080-studio-workshop-editing-and-direct-s3-upload.md) exists, moving between its sections within the same instance must neither self-block nor create two independent active owners. Do not implement the editor redesign as part of this task.
- Acceptance criteria:
    - With A idle or recording in a different browser window and B blocked, confirm takeover only in B. A remains open but deactivated, B becomes active, and no manual action in A is required.
    - Cancellation makes no ownership change. A successful recording handover preserves and reopens the committed recording and leaves no capture tracks active in responsive A.
    - Test A paused, starting/stopping, exporting, saving and failing a write; also test A closing mid-request, duplicate requests, two simultaneous requesters, and repeated takeovers.
    - Test delayed/suspended A resuming after forced revocation: stale writes/finalization are rejected and only B has authority. Failure/timeout is visibly distinct from successful takeover.
    - Retain actual cross-tab lock tests, not only mocked messaging. Document any browser limitations of forced revocation and unverified physical-device behavior.
- Platform reference: [Web Locks lifecycle and the warning about stealing locks](https://www.w3.org/TR/web-locks/#api-lockmanager-request).
