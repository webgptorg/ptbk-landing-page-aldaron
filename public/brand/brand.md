# Promptbook brand kit

Updated: 2026-11-05. Contact: pavol@ptbk.io.

Name: Promptbook. Visual wordmark: promptbook (no trailing dot). CLI: ptbk.
Use the supplied SVG/PNG assets. The PB mark projects onto a regular hexagon:
six equal sides, 120° interior angles. Never stretch, rotate, recolor or add effects.
Blue #17386C on light surfaces; white on dark. Keep at least 1/4 of
the symbol's height clear on all sides. Minimum digital widths: symbol 32 px,
complete lockup 160 px. Headings: Outfit. Body: Inter. Wordmark: outlined Inter Bold.
In the complete lockup, the cube's vertical edge matches the wordmark's cap height.
Logo SVGs contain vector paths, with no embedded bitmap or font dependency.
Editable slide/social templates require Outfit and Inter; their PNG exports do not.

## Czech

Promptbook předává dlouhodobé agendy AI agentům, kteří pracují na Vašem počítači nebo serveru podle Vašich pravidel.

Předáte agendu. Nastavíte pravidla. Spustíte agenty. Pracují sami. Ozvou se, jen když Vás potřebují. Vy máte kdykoliv přehled. Engine běží na Vašem počítači nebo serveru, s Vašimi soubory a modely, které si zvolíte.

## English

Promptbook hands ongoing work to AI agents that run on your computer or server and follow your rules.

Hand over a responsibility. Set the rules. Start your agents. They work independently. They ask only when they need you. You can check in at any time. The engine runs on your computer or server, with your files and your choice of models.
